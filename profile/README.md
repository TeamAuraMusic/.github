- **Playback** — stream and cache from YouTube Music, offline listening, background service, video playback with subtitles and SponsorBlock
- **Audio** — equalizer, normalization, crossfade, tempo and pitch control, skip silence, sleep timer and alarm
- **Lyrics** — word-by-word synced lyrics from seven providers (Kugou, LRCLib, Rush, BetterLyrics, Musixmatch, SimpMusic, Paxsenix) plus translation
- **Beyond playback** — Listen Together sync, offline voice control, Google Cast, AuraCanvas visuals, music recognition, Last.fm scrobbling, Discord Rich Presence
- **Android TV / Google TV** — native Leanback app with D-pad navigation, video playback and a recommendation channel
- **Builds** — `foss` (no Play Services, F-Droid compatible) and `gms` (with Cast), mobile and TV, universal and per-ABI

## Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome in the [AuraMusic repo](https://github.com/TeamAuraMusic/AuraMusic/issues). Translations are community-maintained through [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/auramusic/) — 15 languages, no coding needed.
